1. Overview

ThyroidCA is a browser-based clinical decision support tool for the management of differentiated thyroid carcinoma (DTC). It synthesises the 2025 American Thyroid Association (ATA) guidelines to generate structured, individually tailored management recommendations for use by qualified endocrinologists, endocrine surgeons, nuclear medicine physicians, and medical oncologists.

ThyroidCA is a product of EndoHormone Institute Pty Ltd (EHI), developed and maintained by Professor Steven C. Boyages MBBS FRACP FAFPHM DDU PhD.

What ThyroidCA does

  • Stratifies recurrence risk (Low / Intermediate / High) using ATA 2025 criteria
  • Generates structured recommendations for surgery, RAI, TSH suppression, systemic therapy, and surveillance
  • Extracts clinical data automatically from uploaded pathology reports using AI Pro
  • Saves and retrieves patient cases for later review

What ThyroidCA does not do

  • Diagnose thyroid cancer
  • Replace clinical judgement, MDT discussion, or institutional protocols
  • Interface with electronic medical records or diagnostic equipment
  • Provide advice directly to patients
Important: ThyroidCA is an educational decision support tool for qualified healthcare professionals only. All clinical decisions remain the sole responsibility of the treating clinician.

5. AI Pathology Auto-Fill Pro

ThyroidCA Pro includes an AI-assisted data extraction feature that reads a surgical pathology report and automatically populates the form fields.

How to Use It

  1. Locate the upload bar at the top of the page (below the disclaimer, above the form)
  2. Upload your file using one of two methods:
    • Drag and drop — drag the file directly onto the upload zone
    • Browse — click the Browse button to open a file picker
  3. Supported formats: PDF (digital or scanned), PNG, JPG, TIFF, WebP (max 4 MB)
  4. Once the file is loaded, the filename appears in the bar and the Extract & Fill button activates
  5. Click Extract & Fill — Claude reads the report and populates matching fields (5–15 seconds)
  6. Review all extracted fields — highlighted fields indicate what was auto-populated
  7. Correct any errors, then click Generate Clinical Plan

What Gets Extracted

Patient name, age, tumour size, histology, unfavourable subtype, multifocality and foci count, intrathyroidal status, gross ETE, resection status, margin distance, distant metastasis, vascular invasion (category and foci count), nodal disease (number and largest node size), micrometastases, extranodal extension, and free-text pathology notes (BRAF status, Ki-67, capsular invasion details).

Tips for Best Results

  • Digital PDFs extract best — a text-layer PDF costs ~3× fewer tokens than a scanned image and produces more accurate extraction
  • Limit to the relevant pages — upload the surgical pathology report only, not the full hospital record
  • Compress large files — files over 4 MB will be rejected; use ilovepdf.com to compress before uploading
  • Always verify — AI extraction is highly accurate but not infallible; always review populated fields before generating a plan
Uploaded documents are transmitted via an encrypted server-side proxy to Anthropic's API for processing and are not stored by EHI or Anthropic beyond the duration of the API call. Do not upload documents containing patient identifiers unless you have appropriate consent and institutional privacy compliance.

6. Understanding the Clinical Plan

Click Generate Clinical Plan after entering patient data. The plan appears in the right panel and contains the following sections:

Summary Grid

A row of tiles showing at a glance: patient name, histology, tumour size, unfavourable subtype (if any), focality, serum Tg status, and the ATA 2025 risk tier (colour-coded: green = Low, amber = Intermediate, red = High).

Risk Stratification Drivers

A list of every feature that contributed to the risk tier, with a brief description of each. This makes the reasoning transparent and auditable.

Surgical Management

Recommendation for surgical extent based on risk tier, tumour characteristics, focality, and the procedure already performed. If a lobectomy has been performed, this section addresses whether completion thyroidectomy is recommended.

Radioactive Iodine (RAI)

Intent-based RAI recommendation aligned to ATA 2025:

  • Low risk: RAI not routinely recommended
  • Intermediate risk: Selective — context-dependent, discuss in MDT
  • High risk: Adjuvant RAI recommended (~75–150 mCi)
  • Lobectomy only: RAI flagged as not applicable until completion thyroidectomy
  • Pregnant: Absolute contraindication flagged

TSH Suppression Strategy

Initial TSH target and duration based on risk tier. High risk → <0.1 mU/L; Intermediate → 0.1–0.5 mU/L; Low → low-normal (0.5–2 mU/L). After lobectomy, redirects to replacement rather than suppression.

Systemic / Targeted Therapy

Appears for High risk or M1 disease only. If an actionable driver mutation is selected, the specific targeted agent is recommended (selpercatinib/pralsetinib for RET; larotrectinib/entrectinib for NTRK; dabrafenib + trametinib for BRAF V600E; alectinib for ALK). If no driver, lenvatinib or sorafenib are recommended as MKI options.

Serum Tg Interpretation

A specific interpretation block based on the Tg status selected. After lobectomy, Tg is flagged as uninterpretable as a tumour marker.

Surveillance Protocol

Risk-stratified follow-up schedule including ultrasound, Tg monitoring, and imaging intervals. After lobectomy, switches to ultrasound-based surveillance.

7. Saving and Retrieving Cases

Saving a Case

  1. Generate a clinical plan
  2. Click the Save button (appears in the action row after generating a plan)
  3. The case is stored with the patient name, risk tier, histology, and timestamp
  4. A confirmation toast appears at the bottom right of the screen

Retrieving a Case

  1. Click Saved Cases in the header
  2. The case list shows all saved cases with name, histology, size, risk badge, and date
  3. Click Load & View on any case to restore all form fields and regenerate the plan
  4. Click Delete to permanently remove a case (confirmation required)

9. Troubleshooting & FAQ

AI Extraction

The Extract & Fill button is greyed out
A file must be loaded first. Use Browse or drag a file onto the upload zone. The button activates once the file is read into memory.

I get an error when clicking Extract & Fill

  • File too large — compress the PDF to under 4 MB using ilovepdf.com
  • API key not configured — administrator issue; contact EHI support
  • Rate limit reached — maximum 10 extractions per hour per user; wait and try again
  • Claude returned empty response — the document may be a scanned image with very low resolution; try a higher quality scan or digital PDF

Fields weren't populated after extraction
Some fields may not be present in the report or may be expressed in non-standard language. Review the pathology notes field — the AI records any findings it couldn't map to structured fields there.

The file won't upload
Only PDF, PNG, JPG, TIFF, and WebP formats are supported. Maximum file size is 4 MB. Ensure the file is not password-protected.

Clinical Plan

The risk tier seems too high / too low
Review the Risk Stratification Drivers list — every scoring factor is listed. Verify all entered data matches the pathology report. Correct any inadvertently set fields and regenerate.

RAI shows as 'not applicable'
This appears when hemithyroidectomy/lobectomy is selected and completion thyroidectomy has not been marked as already performed. RAI requires a total or near-total thyroidectomy.

The plan shows no systemic therapy recommendation
Systemic therapy recommendations only appear for High risk or M1 disease.

Subscription

My Pro badge isn't showing after subscribing
Subscription activation normally takes 5–10 seconds. If it hasn't appeared after 30 seconds, refresh the page. If still not activated, click "Already subscribed?" and enter your email address.

I subscribed on another device and can't access Pro
Click "Already subscribed?" in the upgrade prompt and enter the email you used when subscribing.

I was charged but can't access Pro
Contact steve.boyages@gmail.com with your receipt and we will resolve it promptly.

General

Can I use ThyroidCA offline?
The risk engine and plan generator work offline once the page is loaded. AI extraction and saved cases require an internet connection.

Is my patient data stored?
Patient data entered into the form is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to any server except: (a) pathology documents uploaded for AI extraction, transmitted to Anthropic's API and immediately discarded, and (b) case data explicitly saved using the Save button, stored in an encrypted database.

Which browsers are supported?
Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari (current versions). Internet Explorer is not supported.
